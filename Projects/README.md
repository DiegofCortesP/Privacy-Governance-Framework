# NIST CSF & ISO 27001 Security Assessment

## Business Context

A cybersecurity assessment was conducted to evaluate the maturity of information security controls within a customs agency environment.

## Objective

Identify security gaps and establish an improvement roadmap aligned with industry frameworks.

## Assessment Frameworks

- NIST Cybersecurity Framework
- ISO/IEC 27001

## Assessment Areas

### Identify
- Asset visibility
- Risk management
- Critical dependencies

### Protect
- Access management
- Patch management
- Security awareness

### Detect
- Monitoring
- Event analysis

### Respond
- Incident management
- Response planning

### Recover
- Recovery planning
- Business continuity

## Key Findings

- Lack of formal risk management
- Outdated incident response planning
- Weak identity lifecycle controls
- Lack of information classification
- ERP dependency risk
- Insufficient monitoring capabilities

## Maturity Assessment

Current State:
Tier 1 – Partial

Target State:
Tier 2 – Risk Informed

## Deliverables

- Assessment Report
- Security Roadmap
- Governance Recommendations
- Prioritized Improvement Plan
