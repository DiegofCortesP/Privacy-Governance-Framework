**Privacy Compliance Project**



Initial Situation



The organization had no formal privacy governance framework.



**Identified Gaps**



* No Data Protection Officer
* No Personal Data Inventory
* No Records of Processing Activities
* No Incident Management Procedure
* No Data Subject Rights Procedure



**Methodology**

* Governance assessment
* Role definition
* Policy development
* Data inventory
* Processing activity mapping
* Legal basis analysis
* Incident management design



**Deliverables**

* Governance Framework
* Privacy Policy
* Personal Data Inventory
* ROPA / RAT
* Legal Basis Matrix
* Data Subject Rights Procedure
* Incident Management Procedure
