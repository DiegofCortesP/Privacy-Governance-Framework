**Lessons Learned**



*Key Findings*



* Business areas are the primary source of information for identifying processing activities.
* Data inventories should be built from evidence, not assumptions.
* Governance is as important as technology.
* Keeping documentation simple increases adoption.
* Early stakeholder engagement significantly improves data quality.
* Recommendations
* Maintain annual inventory reviews.
* Track new systems and providers.
* Keep version control for all privacy documentation.
* Establish a formal privacy communication channel.
