**Privacy Compliance Implementation Roadmap**

**Phase 1 - Assessment**



**Objectives**

Identify current privacy practices and governance gaps.



**Activities**

* Review existing documentation.
* Identify responsible stakeholders.
* Assess current controls.



**Phase 2 - Governance**

**Activities**



* Appoint a Data Protection Officer.
* Define roles and responsibilities.
* Create governance structure.
* Deliverables
* DPO Appointment
* Roles Matrix
* Governance Model





**Phase 3 - Documentation**

**Activities**



* Develop Privacy Policy.
* Design privacy procedures.
* Create evidence controls.
* Deliverables
* Privacy Policy
* Data Subject Rights Procedure
* Incident Management Procedure





**Phase 4 - Data Mapping**

**Activities**



* Collect information from business areas.
* Identify personal data.
* Consolidate inventories.
* Deliverables
* Personal Data Inventory
* Record of Processing Activities
* Legal Basis Matrix





**Phase 5 - Operationalization**

**Activities**



* Implement registers.
* Define privacy channels.
* Train employees.
* Deliverables
* Incident Register
* Data Subject Request Register
* Training Records





**Phase 6 - Continuous Improvement**

**Activities**



* Annual reviews.
* Inventory updates.
* Control testing.
* Deliverables
* Updated Inventory
* Updated ROPA
* Updated Legal Basis Matrix
