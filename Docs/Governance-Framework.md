**# Privacy Governance Framework**



**## Purpose**



Establish a governance model for privacy and personal data protection programs.



**## Governance Structure**



**### Executive Management**



Responsibilities:



\- Approve privacy policies.

\- Support compliance initiatives.

\- Allocate resources.



**### Data Protection Officer (DPO)**



Responsibilities:



\- Coordinate compliance activities.

\- Maintain privacy documentation.

\- Support business areas.

\- Monitor privacy-related incidents.

\- Manage data subject requests.



**### Business Areas**



Responsibilities:



\- Identify data processing activities.

\- Support inventory updates.

\- Report incidents.

\- Comply with privacy procedures.



**### IT Department**



Responsibilities:



\- Manage technical controls.

\- Implement access controls.

\- Support incident response.

\- Maintain security measures.



**## Governance Components**



\- Privacy Policy

\- Roles and Responsibilities Matrix

\- Personal Data Inventory

\- Record of Processing Activities (ROPA)

\- Legal Basis Matrix

\- Data Subject Rights Procedure

\- Incident Response Procedure

\- Evidence Register



**## Continuous Improvement**



The privacy program should be reviewed periodically and updated whenever:



\- New systems are introduced.

\- New providers are incorporated.

\- New business processes are created.

\- Regulatory requirements change.
