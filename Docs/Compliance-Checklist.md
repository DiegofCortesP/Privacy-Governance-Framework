#### **Privacy Compliance Checklist**



**Governance**

* Data Protection Officer appointed
* Roles and responsibilities documented
* Governance framework defined



**Policies**

* Privacy policy created
* Privacy policy approved
* Policy communicated internally



**Data Mapping**

* Personal Data Inventory created
* Processing activities identified
* Legal basis documented



**Data Subject Rights**

* Rights procedure documented
* Rights register created



**Security Incidents**

* Incident procedure documented
* Incident register created



**Documentation Control**

* Evidence register maintained
* Version control implemented



**Training**

* Privacy awareness training completed
* Attendance evidence maintained



**Continuous Improvement**

* Annual review planned
* Inventory review planned
* Incident review process defined
